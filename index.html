01 / THE CHALLENGE

Plausible alone, inconsistent together

Independent agents can mix actions from different expert strategies. Expressive policies alone do not preserve coordination.

02 / THE IDEA

Learn latents for factorization

A shared latent captures the dependencies needed to make joint expert behavior executable by decentralized policies.

03 / THE OBJECTIVE

Align joint and local flow fields

A density-free surrogate aligns each local vector field with its corresponding component of the centralized joint field.

How it works

Coordination through a shared latent

CoFac-FM learns what agents need to agree on during training, then uses an episode-level latent to guide their local policies during execution.

What should the latent explain?

The latent-conditioned factorization gap measures the divergence between a centralized joint policy and the product of local policies. With sufficiently expressive local policies, the optimal gap separates into residual action dependence and dependence on observations unavailable to each agent.

Why align vector fields?

Evaluating flow-policy densities is expensive. Under the paper’s shared Gaussian interpolation and regularity assumptions, the zero-gap condition is equivalent to joint-to-local field alignment. The practical loss targets this condition without directly estimating the KL gap.
